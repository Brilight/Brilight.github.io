01 — About

Architecture, software and packaging

At the architecture and software levels, I study memory organization, dataflow, compilers and runtime. At the physical level, I work on chiplet placement, thermal and thermo-mechanical simulation, power delivery and signal integrity. I also develop AI-accelerated simulation methods for design evaluation and optimization.

At PicoHeart · Witintech, I work on 3D-IC product architecture for compute-in-memory systems. Before that, I worked in Alibaba DAMO Academy's architecture group on 3.5D-IC systems for LLM inference. During my Ph.D. at Peking University, my research covered multiphysics simulation, thermal-aware placement and AI-accelerated simulation. I have published 15 papers, six as first author, and serve as a reviewer for IEEE TCAD and IEEE TSM.

我主要研究 3D-IC 设计方法，覆盖架构、软件和物理实现，应用于近存计算（3D-DRAM/HBF）、 存内计算（DRAM 和 Flash）以及 NPU、LPU 等其他计算系统。在架构和软件层面，我研究存储组织、数据流、 编译与运行时（runtime）；在物理实现层面，我研究芯粒布局、热与热应力仿真、供电和信号 完整性，也开发 AI 加速仿真方法用于设计评估与优化。目前在字节 · 知存从事面向存内计算的 3D-IC 产品架构工作；此前在阿里达摩院架构组研究面向大模型推理的 3.5D-IC 系统，博士期间 在北京大学研究多物理场仿真、热感知布局和 AI 加速仿真。已发表或录用论文 15 篇 （一作 6 篇），并担任 IEEE TCAD、IEEE TSM 审稿人。
